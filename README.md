# EX No.5.2.DESIGN THE 3D MODEL AS PROVIDED AND CONVERT TO ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW
## DATE:16/11/26

## AIM: 
To create orthographic view of given 3D model

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:

### Step 1: Create the tabletop

1. Open Fusion 360 and select the **Hybrid Design** workspace.
2. Click **Create Sketch** and select the XY plane.
3. <img width="216" height="171" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-11 204524 - Copy (2)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cc821b50-0d7a-470c-a51f-58b6a311da8d" />

4. Choose **Sketch → Rectangle → Center Rectangle**
5. .<img width="435" height="433" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 091820" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e4bf1806-1ed7-489e-800f-2b95d068890d" />

6. 
7. Click on the origin to position the centre of the rectangle.
8. Enter 30 mm as the length and 30 mm as the width.
9. Click **Finish Sketch** to complete the rectangular profile.
10. Select the rectangular profile and click **Extrude**.
11. Enter 4 mm as the extrusion distance.
12. Select **New Component** to create the tabletop as a separate component.
13. Press Enter to complete the tabletop.

### Step 2: Create the corner cut-outs

11. Select the top face of the tabletop and click **Create Sketch**.
12. Choose **Sketch → Rectangle → Two-Point Rectangle**.
13. <img width="435" height="433" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 091820" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d3b70624-27a7-40f1-b886-5fa95ee0272f" />

14. Draw a square profile measuring 2 mm × 2 mm at the first corner of the tabletop.
15. Align the outer edges of the square with the corresponding edges of the tabletop.
16. Repeat the same procedure at the remaining three corners.
17. Click **Finish Sketch**.
18. Select all four square profiles and click **Extrude**.
19. <img width="460" height="617" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-11 212828" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b8518c7b-10c9-4e14-8c20-142db9a07156" />

20. Select **Cut** as the operation and enter 1 mm as the extrusion distance.
21. Press Enter to create the four corner cut-outs.

### Step 3: Create the first leg

20. Click **Create Sketch** and select the appropriate vertical sketch plane.
21. Choose **Sketch → Rectangle → Two-Point Rectangle**.
22. Draw a rectangular profile with a length of 30 mm and a width of 2 mm.
23. Use the **Dimension** tool to set the exact measurements.
24. <img width="562" height="513" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-11 204618" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3fe5b633-e6ca-45a0-ac2a-0783a6d0f2af" />

25. Position the rectangle so that the 30 mm dimension represents the vertical length of the leg.
26. Click **Finish Sketch**.
27. Select the rectangular profile and click **Extrude**.
28. Enter 2 mm as the extrusion distance.
29. Select **New Component** to create the leg as a separate component.
30. Press Enter to complete the first leg.

### Step 4: Position and join the first leg

30. Position the leg component below the first corner of the tabletop.
31. Align the upper face of the leg with the corresponding corner cut-out.
32. Ensure that the leg is properly aligned with the edges of the tabletop.
33. Use the **Join** operation to connect the corresponding faces of the leg and tabletop.
34. <img width="230" height="311" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 090622" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dff0b04a-6902-4cd1-a704-d16fe81c4d3b" />


### Step 5: Create and join the remaining legs

34. Repeat the same sketching procedure to create the second leg using a rectangle measuring 30 mm × 2 mm.
35. Extrude the profile by 2 mm and create it as a separate component.
36. Position the second leg at the opposite corner and join the corresponding faces.
37. Repeat the same procedure to create the third and fourth legs.
38. Ensure that all four legs are positioned at the four corners of the tabletop.
39. Use the **Join** operation to connect the remaining legs to the tabletop.

### Step 6: Final inspection and drawing

40. Use the **Orbit** tool to inspect the completed table from different directions.
41. Verify that the tabletop measures 30 mm × 30 mm with a thickness of 4 mm.
42. Check that all four corner cut-outs measure 2 mm × 2 mm with a depth of 1 mm.
43. Verify that each leg has a length of 30 mm, a width of 2 mm and an extrusion thickness of 2 mm.
44. Ensure that all four legs are properly aligned and joined to the tabletop.
45. Save the completed model.
46. Generate the front, top and side views along with the isometric view using the Drawing workspace.
47. Add the necessary dimensions and hidden lines to complete the engineering drawing.
